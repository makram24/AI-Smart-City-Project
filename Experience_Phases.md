## Customer Experience Roadmap

### Phase 3 – Current Enhancements
- **Neighborhood Playbooks**: Bundle routes, nearby services, and contextual info into reusable micro-guides. Builds on existing routing/places APIs plus caching.
- **Intent-Aware Chat Personas**: Detect commute vs. culture vs. errands, then switch prompts/tone to match user intent, reusing the current AI service pipeline.
- **Mode-Mix Wizard**: Auto-suggest blended itineraries (bike + tram, walk + metro) leveraging routing and public transport modules before larger UI investments.

### Phase 4 – Experience Layer Expansion
- **Dynamic City Moodboard**: Fuse live weather, transport, and events into proactive suggestions (“Rainy afternoon? Cozy cafés + tram timings”). Requires new UI surfaces and notification logic.
- **Augmented Map Stories**: Attach narrative “story cards” (audio/text) to landmarks and surface them when routes pass nearby, turning navigation into a guided tour. Dependent on richer content pipeline and map overlays.

### Phase 5 – Community & Safety Network
- **Community Confidence Signals**: Capture lightweight rider feedback to show live sentiment (“Tram 2 reliable today”). Needs new storage, moderation, and trust scoring.
- **Contextual Safety Layer**: Highlight well-lit streets, construction zones, and accessibility-friendly paths using additional data feeds plus advanced map rendering.

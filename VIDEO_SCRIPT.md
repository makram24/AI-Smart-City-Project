---

### **PROBLEM (0:30 - 1:00)**

**[Visual: Split screen - traditional navigation vs. your app]**

> "Navigating a city shouldn't require juggling multiple apps. You shouldn't need separate tools for maps, transport schedules, weather, and local recommendations. 
> 
> The AI Smart City Project solves this by combining everything into one intelligent interface."

**[Pause 1 second]**

---

### **FEATURE 1: AI CHAT (1:00 - 1:45)**

**[Visual: Full screen chat interface]**

> "The heart of the application is an AI-powered chat interface. Simply ask in natural language..."

**[Action: Type "Find pharmacies near me"]**

> "...'Find pharmacies near me' - and the AI understands your intent, searches real geospatial data, and displays results on an interactive map."

**[Action: Show markers appearing on map]**

> "No complex menus, no learning curve. Let me show you route planning..."

**[Action: Type "Route to Buda Castle"]**

> "...just ask for directions, and the AI calculates the best route, showing it visually on the map with distance and duration."

**[Action: Show route being drawn]**

### **FEATURE 2: INTERACTIVE MAP (1:45 - 2:30)**

**[Visual: Map with various markers]**

> "The interactive map uses real OpenStreetMap data, showing you actual places in Budapest. Each marker is color-coded by type - green for pharmacies, amber for restaurants, blue for transport stops."

**[Action: Click different markers]**

> "Click any marker for detailed information, ratings, and hours. You can filter by category, adjust search radius, and get instant directions to any location."

**[Action: Show map filters, then route to a marker]**

### **FEATURE 3: TRANSPORT (2:30 - 3:15)**

**[Visual: Transport panel open]**

> "Planning your journey is seamless. The app integrates with Budapest's BKK public transport system..."

**[Action: Show nearby stops]**

> "...showing you nearby stops, real-time arrivals, and journey planning."

**[Action: Click a stop, show arrivals]**

> "You can see live vehicle positions, check disruptions, and plan multi-modal routes combining walking, cycling, and public transport."

**[Action: Plan journey, show route]**

## ⚠️ Recording Notes

- **Speak clearly and at moderate pace**
- **Pause 1-2 seconds after each major point**
- **Let features load naturally (don't rush)**
- **If you make a mistake, pause and restart that section**
- **Keep energy level consistent**
